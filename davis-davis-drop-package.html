Heat Pump Installation in Montgomery County, MD
Rebates, Real Costs, and When It Actually Makes Sense

Most homeowners in Montgomery County aren't asking "should I get a heat pump?" They're asking: will it keep my house warm when it's 18 degrees outside, and what will it actually cost me after everything? Those are the right questions. Here's the straight answer.

Maryland Rebates — Real Numbers (2026)
$15,000 Max rebate via EmPOWER Maryland for fuel-switching (gas/oil → electric heat pump) energy.maryland.gov ↗
$7,000 Instant rebates available through BGE & Pepco for qualifying installations pepco.com ↗
$2,000 Federal tax credit (25C) for qualifying heat pump systems — check current eligibility energystar.gov ↗

Rebates require a $100 home energy audit through a certified contractor, pre-approval, and approved equipment. Rebates can be stacked. We walk you through every step before installation begins.

Do Heat Pumps Work in Maryland Winters?

Yes — with the right system design. Maryland winters average 18–35°F in Montgomery County. Modern cold-climate heat pumps are rated to full capacity down to 5°F. The homes we see struggle are ones where the unit was sized for a different climate, or where ductwork was never addressed.

We've installed heat pumps across Kensington, Silver Spring, Bethesda, and Rockville. No two homes are the same. What works in a 1970s split-level in Chevy Chase is different from a newer townhouse in Germantown. We size to the home — not to a catalog spec.

When We Recommend a Heat Pump
  • Replacing a system 15+ years old that's driving up energy bills
  • Switching from oil or propane (largest rebate eligibility)
  • Home with functional ductwork in good condition
  • Owner planning to stay 5+ years (full payback window)
  • BGE or Pepco customer (both utilities have strong rebate programs active now)
When We Don't Recommend One
  • Ductwork is compromised and replacement cost changes the math significantly
  • Home has major insulation or air sealing issues (fix that first — or combine)
  • Short-term ownership horizon where ROI won't close
  • Existing system has years of reliable life left
What a Davis & Davis Installation Includes
  • Manual J load calculation (correct sizing — not guesswork)
  • Rebate eligibility review before you commit to anything
  • Full removal of existing system
  • Installation, commissioning, and performance testing
  • Thermostat integration and full homeowner walkthrough
  • Post-install support — we answer questions after the job is done
